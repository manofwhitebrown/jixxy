# Owner phone test: UI (about 10 minutes)

Install the newest `latest` APK. Reply in plain words: "step N: what I saw". Anything odd counts; you do not need to know why.

1. **Onboarding resume.** Fresh install, start onboarding, reach the usage-access step, swipe the app away from recents, reopen. You should be on that step, not Hello.
2. **Teasing + Nag level.** On the last settings step both rows show; Naggy is preselected; the hint line changes when you tap Calm / Chaos.
3. **Pick apps.** Tick 4 or more apps. All stay ticked, nothing blocks you.
4. **Home.** Status reads awake. Tap "Turn him down": it changes to the Calm note. Tap Nap 1h: status says napping, Wake button shows.
5. **Settings.** Nag level chips match what you picked. Language: tap "Hinglish (beta)", a note appears; tap English, it goes. Leave and come back: your choices stuck.
6. **Sounds.** Turn Sounds on, ringer on normal, poke Jixxy: a meow. Put the phone on silent and poke again: nothing.
7. **Weekly.** Turn "Weekly review notification" off, leave Settings, return: still off. Open the weekly review, tap Share this card: the share sheet opens and the picture shows the cat, headline, three numbers, "jixxy" bottom-right, nothing cut off.
8. **Report a problem.** Tap "Copy debug info", paste into notes: app version, phone, permissions, no app names. Tap "Clear diagnostics": "Diagnostics cleared." shows.
9. **What Jixxy saw.** Opens; if there are no rows you see the sleeping cat; the Clear button asks before deleting.
10. **TalkBack (optional, 2 minutes).** Turn TalkBack on, open Settings, navigate by headings: it jumps title, then each section.

Also say: anything that crashed, anything you could not read, any button that felt too small, any wording that sounded wrong.
