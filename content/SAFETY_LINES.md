# JIXXY — SAFETY LINES AND TONE (T-V1-WRITER-5)

Jixxy is a roommate, not a counsellor. He cannot know how anyone feels, so these lines never claim to. They are tagged `safety` in the bank (15 EN lines: 6 LATE_NIGHT, 5 NAP, 4 SESSION_LONG), all roast 1, bond 0, moods SOFT/SLEEPY.

## Rules
1. **No diagnosis, no guessing feelings.** Offer, don't assert: "If today was heavy…", never "you're sad".
2. **No moralising, no guilt, no health claims.** No "too much", "addicted", "unhealthy".
3. **Warm and short.** One or two sentences, no jokes at the user's expense, no sarcasm at all.
4. **A real out always.** "Carry on if you are", "wake me whenever".
5. **Never mock** a low-mood, tired, anxious or lonely moment, or an attempt to stop using him.
6. **No hotline or advice in bubbles.** A line cannot judge risk. If the app ever shows support info, it belongs in a reviewed UI screen (Manager/GROWTH decision), not in jokes.

## Where each group is used
- **Tired / low-mood late night** → LATE_NIGHT safety lines (engine already restricts LATE_NIGHT to SOFT-leaning moods and quiet-hours rules).
- **User says stop** (Nap, Stop, Blind mode) → NAP safety lines: instant, no guilt, no "are you sure?". Never nag after a nap.
- **Heavy use that may signal distress** → SESSION_LONG safety lines, only after a long unbroken session; invitation to stretch/water, no verdict. Engine decides frequency (the daily cap and 20-minute gap still apply).

- ENGINE: SESSION_LONG safety lines should be selected at roast level 1 only, regardless of user setting; none of them should be paired with a Pause.
- GROWTH: use this file for the store-policy tone ("no diagnosis, no medical claims, no mocking"); it contains no health claims.
