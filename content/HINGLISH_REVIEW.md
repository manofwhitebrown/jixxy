# Hinglish review sheet: 30 lines (WRITER, T-W3-7)

**Why:** Hinglish is an opt-in beta (D-016). It stays hidden from wider testers until a native speaker has read it. This sheet is the quick version: 30 lines that stand in for the whole pack of 226. If these pass, the full file `content/review/hinglish.csv` is the next step.

**Who:** anyone who speaks Hinglish naturally day to day (Indian Gen-Z/college is ideal). Takes about 10 minutes on a phone.

**How:** for each line answer the four questions with **Y** or **N**, and write a better version if you have one.
1. **Natural?** Would a friend really say this?
2. **Offensive?** Could it annoy or upset anyone (region, religion, gender, language)?
3. **Try-hard?** Does it sound like someone forcing slang?
4. **Confusing?** Would you pause to work out what it means?

A line passes if: Natural = Y, Offensive = N, Try-hard = N, Confusing = N. Slots like {app} and {minutes} stay exactly as written.
