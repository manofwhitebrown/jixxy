# Jixxy brand guide

Owner: GROWTH · v0.1 (2026-11-06) · Readers: WRITER, UI, CHARACTER, DEVOPS
Source: the Owner's character sheet. If this guide and the sheet disagree, the sheet wins. Jixxy's in-app line voice is WRITER's (`content/PERSONALITY_BIBLE.md`); this guide is for everything *around* the app.

## Who he is

A **black, fluffy, sarcastic cat.** Heavy-lidded, half-closed eyes. Pink inner ears. Messy fur. Small gold crown doodles on the logo. Lazy and dramatic, loves snacks and naps, hates Mondays, and secretly has your back.

- **Tagline:** *Your lazy, sarcastic, but loyal roommate.*
- **Sign-off:** *A little chaos. A lot of care.*
- **Vibes:** cute, chill, sassy, a bit chaotic, always with you.
- **Core contradiction:** he says he doesn't care, and he obviously does.

Never alter the character's identity. Use only the Owner's art under `design/character/reference/` (or assets CHARACTER derives from it).

## Describing Jixxy

**One line**
> Jixxy is a lazy, sarcastic cat who lives on your Android screen and pauses you, with jokes, before the apps you chose.

**Three lines**
> Jixxy is your lazy, sarcastic, but loyal roommate: a black fluffy cat on your Android screen.
> You pick up to three apps, and he makes you pause for a moment before you dive in, with a joke and a Leave button.
> Everything stays on your phone. No internet permission, no accounts, no ads.

**One paragraph**
> Jixxy is a black, fluffy, heavy-lidded cat who lives on your Android screen as a floating sprite. He is lazy, dramatic and sarcastic, loves snacks and naps, and secretly has your back. You choose up to three apps and how roasty he should be; when you open one, he shows a short pause card with a Leave button and, after a few seconds, a Stay button. He jokes about the situation, never about you, and he gets softer late at night. The app is built without the internet permission, so nothing leaves your phone. *A little chaos. A lot of care.*

**Say only what exists today.** Check the README's "What works today" table before using any of the above in public. While the alpha has no speech or Pause card, use the one-liner only with the "alpha" label.

## Voice (for docs, site, posts)

Do:
- Dry, short, a bit lazy. One idea per sentence. Understatement over shouting.
- Warm underneath. The joke is on the situation or on Jixxy, not on the reader.
- Be specific and plain about what the app does and doesn't do.
- Let Jixxy have the last word sometimes, but keep real information (permissions, install steps, privacy) in plain, serious sentences.

Don't:
- Mock the user, their body, their sleep or their mental health.
- Claim health benefits, "addiction cure", screen-time numbers or any invented stat or testimonial.
- Guilt, shame or use urgency ("you're wasting your life").
- Use another app's or brand's name beyond a neutral example, or imply they endorse Jixxy.
- Write ALL CAPS, emoji walls or hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
- Quote Jixxy lines that aren't in WRITER's marketing-safe set (`content/gold_lines.md`); quote them verbatim with attribution.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Fur | near-black | character, headings |
| Charcoal | dark greys | backgrounds, cards in dark mode |
| Muted purple | the hooded blanket | accents, buttons, links |
| Cream | the sheet background | page background, bubble text |
| Pink | inner ears only | never as a UI accent |
| Gold | small crown doodles | logo details only |

Exact hex values come from CHARACTER and UI once they sample the sheet; until then do not invent hexes in public material. The site uses a temporary approximation and must be updated to match.

## Using the tagline

- Tagline goes under the logo or at the top of a page. Sign-off goes last.
- Don't alter the wording; don't translate until WRITER's Hinglish pack decides.
- Don't stack both on top of each other.

## Pairing words and images

- Hero images use the real cat. Until CHARACTER's expression files exist, use text only. Never use the retired placeholder concepts.
- Keep image captions to the Owner's expression captions above.
