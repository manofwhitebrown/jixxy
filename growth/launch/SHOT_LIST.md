# Screen-recording shot list (T-G3-7)

One page for the Owner. **On hold:** record only after a build is stable (the Pause works on your phone, he doesn't vanish) and the Owner says go. Nothing here is posted or dated. Rules: see `README.md` in this folder.

## Before you record (5 minutes)
- Clean test profile or cleaned phone: clear notifications, hide your name, contacts and chats. Do not-disturb on.
- Use a **neutral dummy app** or crop/blur logos for the Pause shots. Never imply another brand endorses Jixxy.
- Install the build, finish setup, choose a roast level, set one dummy app as the Pause app.
- Phone screen recorder, portrait, 1080p, no music (or CC0 only). Record each shot 2 or 3 times; keep the best.
- Note the build's "Built" time next to each clip so we know what's on screen.

## The shots (each 5 to 10 seconds, raw)

| # | Shot | What to do | What must be visible | Reel script it feeds* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pause card** | Open the dummy Pause app | Card appears with Leave and Stay; his face peeks | Pause script |
| 2 | **Leave** | Tap Leave on a fresh card | Instant jump to Home screen | Pause script |
| 3 | **Stay** | Tap Stay on a new card, wait for the ring | Countdown ring, then Stay unlocks and closes | Pause script |
| 4 | **Bubble** | Switch apps / plug in the charger until he speaks | Speech bubble types out; 😂 / 😴 bar if shown | Charger / low-battery scripts |
| 5 | **Drag** | Drag him across the screen, let go, rotate phone | He stays on screen; taps pass through elsewhere | Intro |
| 6 | **Nap** | Tap Nap | He goes quiet and sleepy; "Nap time..." mood | Nap script |
| 7 | **Blanket / wake** | Wake him again after a nap | He's back and awake | Nap script |
| 8 | **Setup** | Pick up to 3 apps; try a 4th | The 4th is refused; roast-level picker | Setup / privacy |
| 9 | **Weekly card** | Open the weekly review | The share card, with a harmless dummy week | Weekly script |
| 10 | **Permission truth** | Settings, Apps, Jixxy, Permissions | No internet permission visible in app info | Privacy script |

*Names are loose; match to `SCRIPTS.md` when you edit.

## Don't film
Real chats, banking or other protected apps, other people's content, a low-battery or late-night moment staged to mock the viewer, anything showing sleep, bodies or mental health as a joke. Late-night lines only as quiet soft visuals.

## After recording
Send clips to the team through the Owner's usual channel, with phone model, Android version and build time. GROWTH will cut captions from `CAPTIONS.md`. Posting dates and platforms are the Owner's call; no tracking links, only the Releases page or Pages site.
