# Reels / Shorts scripts (rebased on the cat, T-G2-5)

**Scripts only. Nothing here is filmed or posted.** Film only when the build actually does what the script shows: cat art on screen, bubbles with real lines, the Pause card. Today's alpha does none of that.

Format: 15 to 30 s, vertical 9:16, screen recording on a real phone. Hook in the first 2 seconds, plain on-screen text, voice-over optional. Don't fake reactions: if a line doesn't appear, record again. Clear notifications, crop or blur other apps' logos, no private info on screen.

**Where words come from**
- *In-app lines* are quoted unedited from WRITER's marketing-safe set (`content/gold_lines.md`).
- *Sheet captions* (`...really?`, `Respect.` and so on) are the Owner's expression captions. They are the art's wording, not necessarily in-app lines. Where a script needs a line that isn't in the bank (dropped, blanket), it is marked **[NEEDS LINE: WRITER]**. Use the sheet caption on the card art only, and ask WRITER before showing it as a live bubble.

---

## 1. "...really?" (about 15 s) — the stare

- **0-2 s (hook):** Text: "My phone has a roommate. He is not impressed." Close-up of the half-lidded stare expression (`...really?`) at the screen edge.
- **2-8 s:** Open a chosen app. Cat wakes, Pause card slides up. Hold on a gold line: "Leave now, or I start rating the thumbnails."
- **8-12 s:** Show **Leave** and **Stay** buttons. Tap Leave.
- **12-15 s:** Text: "He pauses me before apps I picked." End card (tagline).
- **Needs:** cat art for the stare state, real Pause card.

## 2. "YOU DROPPED ME?! Rude." (about 15 s)

- **0-2 s (hook):** Text: "Never drop the cat."
- **2-9 s:** Drag the sprite across the screen and let go. He falls and reacts (the shocked/dropped expression).
- **9-13 s:** Bubble. **[NEEDS LINE: WRITER]** The sheet caption is "YOU DROPPED ME?! Rude."
- **13-15 s:** Text: "He has feelings. Mostly about gravity." End card.
- **Needs:** the dropped state wired to a line; verified drag on a device.

## 3. "5%?! bro charge." (about 20 s) — low battery and charger

- **0-2 s (hook):** Text: "He panics before you do."
- **2-8 s:** Battery low on a real phone (or a genuine low-battery moment). Cat shows the low-battery expression. Bubble from the bank, for example: "Battery at {battery}%. We're close to the dark, my friend." (use the real number shown on screen).
- **8-14 s:** Plug in the charger. Cat shows the charging expression. Bubble: "Charger on. I'm sitting in the sun. Metaphorically."
- **14-20 s:** Text: "Charger on. Everybody calm down." End card.
- **Needs:** `LOW_BATTERY` and `CHARGING` states wired through the brain (D-010); a real battery moment, not edited.

## 4. "Respect." (about 20 s) — rare praise

- **0-2 s (hook):** Text: "He almost never compliments you."
- **2-8 s:** Open a chosen app, Pause card appears, tap **Leave** right away.
- **8-14 s:** Cat in pixel sunglasses (`Respect.`). Bubble: "Respect. That was smooth."
- **14-20 s:** Text: "Rare. Earned. He means it." End card.
- **Needs:** Pause card, Leave reaction, sunglasses state.

## 5. "We should both sleep..." (about 15 s) — the blanket, quiet

- **0-2 s (hook):** Calm dim screen. No text overlay, no joke.
- **2-10 s:** Cat in the purple hooded blanket at the screen edge, eyes drifting shut. Optional text: "Nap time..." (sheet caption). **[NEEDS LINE: WRITER]** for "We should both sleep..." before using it as a bubble.
- **10-15 s:** One tap puts him to nap (shows the one-tap nap). Text: "One tap and he goes quiet." End card.
- **Rules:** no voice-over, no jokes about the viewer's sleep, being up late or mental health. This is about *him* napping, not about you. Don't use it as a late-night targeting ad.
- **Needs:** nap/wake verified on a device (the alpha had a nap/stop/wake bug).

## Not used (on purpose)

- *"nine times in a row? seek help."* and *"Another 2 hours on reels? Legend. 💀"* are mock-up captions on the sheet. The first touches mental-health territory and the second names another brand and implies a screen-time number. Don't use either in ads or posts without the Owner's say-so.
