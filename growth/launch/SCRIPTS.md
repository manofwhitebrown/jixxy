# Reels / Shorts scripts

All five are 15 to 30 seconds, vertical 9:16, screen recording of the real app on a real phone. Hook in the first 2 seconds. All on-screen text is plain; spoken voice-over is optional.

**Before filming:** a working build, Jixxy set to up to 3 apps, notifications cleared, no private info visible, logos of other apps cropped or blurred, screen recording at 60 fps if the phone supports it. Don't fake the sprite's reactions; if a line doesn't appear, record again.

Jixxy's lines below are quoted unedited from `content/gold_lines.md` (marketing-safe set).

---

## 1. "He pauses you" (about 20 s)

- **0 to 2 s (hook):** Text: "My phone has a roommate now." Sprite dozing at the edge of a home screen.
- **2 to 6 s:** Tap a video app you chose. Sprite wakes. Pause card slides up.
- **6 to 12 s:** Card reads: "Leave now, or I start rating the thumbnails." Hold on it. Show the **Leave** and **Stay** buttons.
- **12 to 17 s:** Tap Leave. Card closes, back to home screen.
- **17 to 20 s:** Text: "He pauses me before apps I picked." End card: "Jixxy. Free for Android. Early tester build."
- **Caption:** see `CAPTIONS.md`, set A.

## 2. "Leave or stay: both are fine" (about 25 s)

- **0 to 2 s (hook):** Text: "He can't stop you. He can only make you pause."
- **2 to 8 s:** Open a chosen app. Pause card: "Pause: will this still sound good tomorrow?" (shopping app, cropped).
- **8 to 14 s:** Tap **Stay**. Show the short wait before Stay unlocks if it's visible. Jixxy: "Fine. Five minutes. I'll be napping, loudly."
- **14 to 22 s:** Second take, different app: tap **Leave**. Jixxy: "Respect. That was smooth."
- **22 to 25 s:** Text: "Your choice, every time." End card as above.
- **Why:** shows there's no guilt, no lock, always an out. Don't edit lines.

## 3. "Meet the roommate" (about 15 s)

- **0 to 2 s (hook):** Text: "Say hi to the smallest roommate." Sprite appears for the first time after setup.
- **2 to 7 s:** Bubble: "Oh good, a roommate. I call the sofa."
- **7 to 12 s:** Poke the sprite a few times. Bubble: "Poke me once more and I file a complaint."
- **12 to 15 s:** Text: "Tiny. Dramatic. Secretly on your side." End card.
- **Why:** pure character, no scrolling theme, easy to share.

## 4. "Check it yourself: no internet permission" (about 25 s)

- **0 to 2 s (hook):** Text: "This app has no internet permission. Check."
- **2 to 10 s:** Phone Settings → Apps → Jixxy → Permissions. Show the permission list on screen, slowly. Real screen only.
- **10 to 18 s:** Voice-over or text: "No internet permission. No accounts. No ads. No analytics. Everything stays on your phone."
- **18 to 25 s:** Show the two permissions he does ask for (Display over other apps, Usage access) and say why: "to float on screen, and to know which app is in front."
- **Before posting:** re-verify the live manifest and `PRIVACY.md`; shoot only on the actual build you're releasing. Don't claim more than what's shown (no "100% secure", no "unhackable", no "can never go online").
- **Why:** trust is a feature; it's verifiable by anyone.

## 5. "Rare praise" (about 20 s)

- **0 to 2 s (hook):** Text: "He almost never compliments you."
- **2 to 8 s:** Open a chosen app, pause card appears, tap **Leave** right away.
- **8 to 14 s:** Bubble: "Left without a fight. That was genuinely good."
- **14 to 20 s:** Text: "Rare. Earned. He means it." End card.
- **Optional soft ending (quiet visual only):** dim, calm screen with the line "The world's gone quiet. Take your time." No voice-over, no joke about sleep or being up late, no targeting.
- **Why:** shows he rewards your choices instead of guilting you.
