## Rules for everything in this folder

1. **Honest.** Say what the app does: it pauses you before the apps you choose, with jokes. No "reduces screen time by X%", no "cures addiction", no wellbeing or treatment claims, no "digital detox" framing.
2. **No fake proof.** No invented testimonials, user counts, ratings, downloads or statistics. If a tester gives a quote, use it only with their explicit permission, and attribute it as they prefer.
3. **Say it's pre-release** wherever there's room. Testers will hit rough edges.
4. **Lines are WRITER's.** Quote the marketing-safe lines from `content/gold_lines.md` unedited, attributed to Jixxy. Don't write new roast lines in Jixxy's voice; ask WRITER. Captions and posts below are written in a plain narrator voice, not Jixxy's.
5. **Never mock users.** Jokes are about the situation, not the viewer. Don't target people who are up late, and don't joke about sleep, bodies or mental health. Late-night lines appear only as quiet, soft visuals.
6. **No third-party brand implication.** Don't show or name other apps in a way that implies endorsement or comparison. Crop or blur logos, or use a neutral dummy app.
7. **Privacy claims must be literally true.** Before posting any "no internet permission" content, re-check the current manifest and `PRIVACY.md`.
8. **No private data on screen.** Clear notifications, hide names and contacts, use a clean test phone or profile.
9. **Music:** none, or CC0 only. No commercial tracks.
10. **No tracking links.** Link to the GitHub Releases page or the Pages site only.
